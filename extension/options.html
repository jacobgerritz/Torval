Torval

A pop-up dictionary that runs entirely on this machine. No account, no telemetry, no server; nothing you read or look up is sent anywhere.

GPL-3.0, at github.com/jacobgerritz/Torval. The dictionaries keep their own licences, below.

Dictionaries

One a language, read in when you first choose that language. Frequency and pitch accent are part of each, not separate files.
