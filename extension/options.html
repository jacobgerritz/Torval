Recording from video

Seconds kept before the line.

On copy-protected video, click the Torval button in the toolbar once per tab. The browser allows no picture or sound until you do.

Netflix's lines are read off the screen, so its subtitles have to be showing. Torval asks the player to turn them on; where that does not take, choose the language in the player's own menu.

Torval

A pop-up dictionary that runs entirely on this machine. No account, no telemetry, no server; nothing you read or look up is sent anywhere.

GPL-3.0, at github.com/jacobgerritz/Torval. The dictionaries keep their own licences, below.

Free, with no paid version behind it. Support the work if you would like to.

Dictionaries

One a language, read in when you first choose that language. Frequency and pitch accent are part of each, not separate files.

When something goes wrong

Narrates finding and reading subtitles. Send it with a bug report.
