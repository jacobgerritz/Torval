Gets the words you already know out of your collection, as a text file, on
the clipboard, or as JSON.

**Tools → Export Known Words…**

Pick a deck and a field, tick the card states you want, and it reads that
field off every card in them. Mature, young, learning and relearning are
all on by default. New cards never are: a card you have not seen is not a
word you have studied. One deck at a time, since a list with two languages
in it is not much use.

A field usually holds more than the word, so HTML, sound tags, cloze
markers and entities come out, and Japanese furigana (`漢字[かんじ]`) is
reduced to the kanji. One tick box drops a leading *il*, *la*, *el* or
*un*, for decks that store a noun with its article.

Words come out oldest first, in a file named for the deck and the day.

The third button writes JSON with the date each word was first studied,
which a text file cannot carry.
[Torval](https://github.com/jacobgerritz/Torval), a pop-up dictionary that
keeps a list of the words you know, reads it under **Settings → Words**.
