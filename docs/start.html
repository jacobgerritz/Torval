Getting started

Written for people who have never installed a browser add-on or opened Anki. Nothing here assumes you know what any of it means. If you get stuck at a step, that step is the problem, not you.

You can stop after part two. Torval is useful as a plain dictionary, and everything after that is optional. Parts three and four are for people who want to remember the words they look up.

Part 1. Install it

  1. Open the store page for your browser

    Firefox users want the Firefox Add-ons page; Chrome, Edge, Brave and Opera users want the Chrome Web Store page. Both are linked from the front page. Click Add to Firefox or Add to Chrome and confirm.

  2. Pin the button to your toolbar

    After installing you will see a small puzzle-piece icon near the top right of the browser. Click it, find Torval, and click the pin beside it. The Torval button now sits in your toolbar where you can reach it. You need it for video later, and it is the quickest way into the settings.

  3. Say which language you are reading

    Click the Torval button. It asks once, on the first run. Pick the language you are learning, not the one you already speak.

    Above it is Your language, the one you already have. It is set to English, and it decides what the list below it can offer, because a dictionary explains one language in another. Spanish speakers should change it first: see part five.

  4. Wait about a minute

    The dictionary is copying itself into your browser. A percentage counts up on the toolbar button. This happens once, not every time, and afterwards Torval works with your internet off.

Part 2. Look words up

  1. Open a page in that language

    Anything at all: a news site, a blog, a Wikipedia article. Not a PDF and not a picture of text, since Torval reads the words on the page rather than looking at it.

  2. Hold Shift and point at a word

    Do not click. Hold the Shift key down and move the mouse over a word. A small box appears next to it with what the word means. Let go of Shift and the box goes away.

    Nothing happening? The likeliest reason is that the page is in a different language from the one you picked. Torval stays quiet rather than guessing.

  3. Read what the box tells you

    The word in its dictionary form at the top, what kind of word it is, then the meanings in order. If the word on the page was a changed form, walked or hablábamos, the box shows the plain form it comes from.

That is the whole of the basic use. No account, nothing to set up, nothing sent anywhere. If that is all you wanted, you are finished.

Part 3. Anki, if you want to keep the words

Anki is a separate, free program for memorising things. It shows you a word, you say whether you remembered it, and it works out when to show it again. Torval can put words into it in one click, but Anki has to be installed first, and it has to be running at the moment you click.

  1. Install Anki

    From apps.ankiweb.net. It is free on computers. Open it once and let it create your first deck.

    This has to be the computer version. The phone apps cannot talk to a browser add-on. Your cards will sync to your phone afterwards.

  2. Install AnkiConnect inside Anki

    This is the part that lets Torval and Anki speak to each other. In Anki: Tools, then Add-ons, then Get Add-ons…, and paste this code into the box: 

    2055492159

    Click OK, then restart Anki. Nothing visible changes, which is correct. AnkiConnect has no window of its own.

  3. Make a note type, or use the one we suggest

    A note type is the shape of a card: which boxes it has, and what the front and back look like. If you already have one you like, keep it. If not, this page walks through making one that Torval fills in completely.

  4. Point Torval at it

    With Anki open, click the Torval button, then the settings, then Anki. Choose your deck and your note type. Torval matches up the boxes by itself. Press Save.

    A message saying Anki is not answering almost always means Anki is closed, or AnkiConnect was installed without restarting Anki afterwards.

  5. Make your first card

    Back on a page, hold Shift, hover a word, and click the + in the corner of the box. The card appears in Anki straight away, with the sentence you found the word in.

Part 4. Video, if you watch in that language

On YouTube and Netflix, Torval reads the subtitles and works out when each line is spoken. The line appears under the video where you can hover the words in it, A steps back a line and D steps forward, and a card made from a subtitle can carry the picture and the sound of the line itself.

Two things protected services ask of you.

Click the Torval button once per tab when you open a video on Netflix. Without that click the browser will not let any add-on take a picture or record sound, so the card arrives without them.

The subtitles have to be switched on in the player, in the language you are learning. Torval tries to turn them on for you; where that does not take, choose them in the player's own menu.

Recording the sound from a protected video works in Chrome only. Firefox has never given add-ons access to a tab's audio. Everything else works the same in both.

Part 5. If you would rather not read English

Open the settings and change Your language, at the top left, to Español. Everything is then in Spanish, and Inglés appears as a language you can read.

That is the one dictionary here written the other way round: English words explained in Spanish, some seventy thousand of them. A word nobody has written up in Spanish is left alone rather than answered in English, so it does not appear when you hover it and does not count against your score on the page.

Japanese and Italian are not offered to a Spanish speaker, because nobody has written them up in Spanish and explaining them in English would be pretending otherwise. English speakers can read English too, which gives a monolingual dictionary.

When something is not working

No box when I hold Shift

The page is probably in a different language from the one you picked, or the dictionary is still loading. Check the toolbar button: a percentage there means it is still working.

The box appears but says nothing useful

Names, brand names and foreign words are not in any dictionary. Press 3 to tell Torval to stop counting that word against you.

Anki says it is not answering

Anki has to be open on the same computer, with AnkiConnect installed and Anki restarted since. Those three together fix nearly every case.

The card has no picture or sound

On a protected video, click the Torval button in the toolbar once for that tab, then make the card again. The card itself says which of the two was missing.

Still stuck? Open an issue and say what you did and what happened. It helps more than you would think.
