Keys

ShiftHold and point at a word to look it up
2Mark the word as known
1Mark it as not known again
3Ignore it, for names and the like
A DPrevious and next subtitle line
SFast-forward the parts where nobody speaks
BFind the word in Anki

You can change any of them in Torval's settings, under Keys.

Questions

How do I open the settings?

Click the Torval button in your browser's toolbar, then Settings.

What's the percentage at the top of the page?

How much of the page you already know. It's off until you turn it on in Settings → Words, and gets more useful as you mark words as known.

Nothing pops up. What's wrong?

The first time, Torval needs about a minute to set up its dictionary. After that, check that Torval is switched on in the toolbar button and that you chose the language the page is in.

Does it work on Netflix?

Yes. Turn on Netflix's subtitles in the language you're learning, and Torval can read them. For cards with a picture, click the Torval button in the toolbar once on that tab first. Sound from Netflix only works in Chrome.

How do I make Anki cards?

Follow the Anki setup. After that, press + in the popup.

Will I lose my words?

Torval saves a copy to your Downloads folder, in Torval, once a day. To bring them back, use Load from a file in Settings → Words.
