A note type for Torval

If you already have a note type you like, keep it. Torval works with whatever you have. This is for people who do not.

It makes a sentence card: the line you met the word in on the front, the word and what it means on the back. Every field is optional in the templates, so a card with no picture or no pitch accent simply does not show those rows rather than leaving a gap.

The field names below are the ones Torval recognises, so once the note type exists, Settings → Anki fills the mapping in by itself and you have nothing to match up by hand.

1. Make the note type

In Anki: Tools → Manage Note Types → Add → Add: Basic, and call it Torval.

2. The fields

Fields…, then add these, exactly as spelled. Delete Basic's own Front and Back once the others are in. Set Sort by this field on Word. 

Word
Reading
Sentence
Definition
Word Audio
Sentence Audio
Pitch
Stress
Image
Sentence Before
Sentence After

Reading and Pitch are Japanese; Stress is Italian and Spanish. Leaving in the ones your language does not use costs nothing, and means the same note type works if you take up another.

3. The front

Cards…, then paste this over the front template.

<div class="sentence">{{Sentence}}</div>

{{#Image}}<div class="shot">{{Image}}</div>{{/Image}}
{{#Sentence Audio}}<div class="play">{{Sentence Audio}}</div>{{/Sentence Audio}}

4. The back

{{FrontSide}}

<hr id="answer">

<div class="word">
  {{Word}}{{#Reading}} <span class="reading">{{Reading}}</span>{{/Reading}}
  {{#Word Audio}}<span class="play">{{Word Audio}}</span>{{/Word Audio}}
</div>

{{#Pitch}}<div class="accent">{{Pitch}}</div>{{/Pitch}}
{{#Stress}}<div class="accent">{{Stress}}</div>{{/Stress}}

<div class="meaning">{{Definition}}</div>

{{#Sentence Before}}<div class="around">{{Sentence Before}}</div>{{/Sentence Before}}
{{#Sentence After}}<div class="around">{{Sentence After}}</div>{{/Sentence After}}

6. Point Torval at it

Settings → Anki, choose your deck and then Torval as the note type. The fields match themselves. Press + on a word and the card arrives filled in.

No recording for a word? Italian and Spanish pronunciations come from volunteers, and they have not read everything. Adding {{tts it_IT:Word}} to the back template makes Anki say the word itself, with the voices already on your phone or computer. Use es_ES or ja_JP as it suits.
