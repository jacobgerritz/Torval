Torval

Point at a word. See what it means.

A free pop-up dictionary for Japanese, Italian and Spanish. It works on any website, on YouTube and on Netflix.

Add to Firefox Add to Chrome

What it does

Explains any word

Even when it's conjugated: hablábamos finds hablar.

Helps with videos

Look up words in the subtitles on YouTube and Netflix, and replay a line with one key.

Makes flashcards

One click puts a word on an Anki card, with the sentence, and the picture and sound from a video.

Tracks your progress

If you like, it shows how much of a page or video you already understand.

How to use it

  1. Add it to your browser

    Then choose the language you're learning. That's all the setup there is.

  2. Hold Shift and point at a word

    Its meaning pops up. Let go and it disappears.

  3. Make a flashcard, if you use Anki

    Press + in the popup. Setting up Anki takes a few minutes, once.
